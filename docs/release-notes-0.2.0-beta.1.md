# LongTable 0.2.0-beta.1

LongTable 0.2 beta reduces the provider surface to two skills and introduces a
real completion boundary for journal-grounded research production.

## Public surface

- `$longtable` is the research diagnosis and Research Brief front door.
- `$longtable-research` is the journal-to-artifact execution skill.
- `longtable research` and `@longtable/research` are canonical.
- `longtable assure` names the former Panel harness as Research Assurance.
- Deprecated full, start, interview, panel, per-role, and scholar-research
  routes remain compatibility inputs for one release but do not install extra
  skills.

## Verified Research Package

A run cannot report `completed` without:

- versioned Research Brief JSON and Markdown
- lawful access plan with typed outcomes
- human-accepted target-journal topic and format profile
- search, full-text, evidence, claim, and synthesis artifacts
- human-reviewed editable visual portfolio
- five-boundary Research Assurance record
- limitations, reproduction command, hashes, human README, and passing
  verification report

## Visual boundary

Research tables, figures, and diagrams are deterministic, editable, and bound
to data and provenance. Image generation is permitted only for clearly labeled
brand or concept artwork and must not fabricate evidence-bearing visuals.

## Beta promotion rule

Promotion requires at least three complete matched prospective E2E pairs, zero
hard-gate failures, non-worse time to an accepted package, and improvement in at
least one burden or trust measure. Stages that add friction without marginal
value are removed or made opt-in.
